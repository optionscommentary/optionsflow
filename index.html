About Us

Who We Are

Options Commentary is an independent research and analytics platform focused on Indian index options markets. We are a team of passionate market analysts and quantitative researchers who believe that institutional-grade dealer flow analysis should be accessible to serious retail participants.

We are not a brokerage, investment advisor, or SEBI-registered entity. We are analysts who share our work publicly.

What We Do

We run a proprietary Gamma Exposure Model (GEXMA) that processes publicly available option chain data for Indian indices — NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, SENSEX, FINNIFTY, and MIDCAP NIFTY — and produces the following analytics:

  • GEX (Gamma Exposure) — dealer hedging pressure at each strike
  • VEX (Vanna Exposure) — sensitivity of dealer delta to volatility changes
  • CEX (Charm Exposure) — time-decay effect on dealer delta hedging
  • ITM VEX — vanna exposure from in-the-money strikes
  • Probable Positioning — synthesised view of likely dealer and institutional positioning
  • Volatility Surface — 3D implied volatility across strikes and expiries
  • PCR, Vol Skew, OI Analysis — complementary flow and sentiment indicators

All analytics are updated periodically during market hours (09:15–15:30 IST, Monday–Friday) and published to this public dashboard.

Our Philosophy

Markets are largely driven by dealer hedging flows, not just directional sentiment. By understanding where dealers are forced to buy and sell to hedge their options books, participants can better anticipate short-term price behaviour around key strikes and levels.

We do not provide trade recommendations or buy/sell signals. We provide the analytical framework — you draw your own conclusions.
