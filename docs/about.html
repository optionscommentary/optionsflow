γ

Gamma Exposure (GEX)

Measures how much buying or selling market makers are forced to do per 1% move in the index. The single most important structural force in modern options markets.

ν

Vanna Exposure (VEX)

Tracks how dealer delta hedges shift when implied volatility changes. Explains systematic flows that appear random to price-chart traders.

Δ

Charm Exposure (CEX)

Measures how delta hedges decay with time. Responsible for the persistent directional pressure seen in the final days of every expiry cycle.

◈

Dealer Positioning

Estimates whether market makers are long or short gamma at each strike — strike by strike — using OI change, IV direction and moneyness signals.

⬡

Volume Flow Analytics

Session volume, Volume/OI Ratio (VOR) per strike, Volume PCR vs OI PCR divergence, hottest strike detection and wall quality tagging — separating fresh conviction from stale legacy positions.

Background
Why This Platform Exists

The Indian F&O market is one of the most active options markets in the world by volume — yet the structural analytics that professional desks use to navigate it are almost entirely absent from the retail and independent trader's toolkit. Charts, indicators and price-action techniques dominate the conversation. Dealer mechanics, gamma exposure and volatility structure rarely do.

OptionsFlow was built to change that. The entire platform — the Python analytics engine, the data pipeline, the dashboard — was built from scratch to bring institutional-grade structural analysis to anyone trading Nifty, BankNifty, Sensex and other NSE & BSE indices. The goal is simple: give independent traders the same structural picture that market makers and large desks see.

All data is derived from publicly available NSE and BSE option chain data and standard Black-Scholes pricing. The analysis is systematic, rule-based and updated regularly throughout the trading day.

How It Works
The Methodology

Every output on the dashboard is generated by a systematic, rule-based Python engine applied to live NSE and BSE option chain data. No manual analysis. No editorial opinion. Pure structural mechanics.

  • Data SourcePublicly available NSE and BSE option chain feeds, updated throughout the trading session.
  • GEX EngineBlack-Scholes gamma computed for all strikes across all expiries. Aggregated by dealer posture assumption across four scenarios.
  • Prob. GEXStrike-by-strike dealer positioning estimated using a proprietary multi-factor model.
  • VEX / CEXVanna and charm computed per strike, aggregated across the estimated dealer book, giving a fuller picture of dealer hedging pressure as expiry approaches.
  • Vol SurfaceFull implied volatility surface across strikes and expiries, plotted as an interactive 3D chart.
  • Volume FlowSession call/put volume per strike, Volume/OI Ratio (VOR) conviction scoring, Volume PCR vs OI PCR divergence, and hottest strike identification. Active vs stale structural level tagging.
