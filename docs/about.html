About

Independent Options Analytics
for Indian Index Traders

OptionsFlow is a free, independent platform that decodes the structural forces driving Nifty, BankNifty and other NSE indices — using the same dealer flow mechanics that professional options desks analyse every day.

γ

Gamma Exposure (GEX)

Measures how much buying or selling market makers are forced to do per 1% move in the index. The single most important structural force in modern options markets.

ν

Vanna Exposure (VEX)

Tracks how dealer delta hedges shift when implied volatility changes. Explains systematic flows that appear random to price-chart traders.

Δ

Charm Exposure (CEX)

Measures how delta hedges decay with time. Responsible for the persistent directional pressure seen in the final days of every expiry cycle.

◈

Dealer Positioning

Estimates whether market makers are long or short gamma at each strike — strike by strike — using OI change, IV direction and moneyness signals.

Background
Why This Platform Exists

The Indian F&O market is one of the most active options markets in the world by volume — yet the structural analytics that professional desks use to navigate it are almost entirely absent from the retail and independent trader's toolkit. Charts, indicators and price-action techniques dominate the conversation. Dealer mechanics, gamma exposure and volatility structure rarely do.

OptionsFlow was built to change that. The entire platform — the Python analytics engine, the data pipeline, the dashboard — was built from scratch to bring institutional-grade structural analysis to anyone trading Nifty, BankNifty and other NSE indices. The goal is simple: give independent traders the same structural picture that market makers and large desks see.

All data is derived from publicly available NSE option chain data and standard Black-Scholes pricing. The analysis is systematic, rule-based and updated regularly throughout the trading day.

How It Works
The Methodology

Every output on the dashboard is generated by a systematic, rule-based Python engine applied to live NSE option chain data. No manual analysis. No editorial opinion. Pure structural mechanics.

  • Data SourcePublicly available NSE option chain feeds, updated throughout the trading session.
  • GEX EngineBlack-Scholes gamma computed for all strikes across all expiries. Aggregated by dealer posture assumption across four scenarios.
  • Prob. GEXStrike-by-strike dealer positioning estimated using five signals: IV direction, OI change, moneyness, ATM proximity and recency weighting.
  • VEX / CEXVanna and charm computed per strike, aggregated across the estimated dealer book. Most meaningful in the final 5 days before expiry.
  • Vol SurfaceFull implied volatility surface across strikes and expiries, plotted as an interactive 3D chart.
