FAQ
Common Questions
Who is this for?
Anyone who trades or wants to trade NSE F&O options — whether you're just starting out or already active. The full mentorship starts from basics and builds up. The trial session is better suited for someone who has at least traded options a few times and wants to see how flow analysis fits into their process.
Do I need to know options basics before joining?
For the full mentorship — no. We cover everything from the ground up, including how options work, Greeks, and how to read the dashboard. For the trial session — a basic familiarity with calls and puts helps, but it's not mandatory. Just tell me your current level when you message and we'll start from the right place.
How do I pay?
Payment details are shared over WhatsApp once we confirm a slot. UPI accepted. Trial session is ₹999. Optionsflow Mentorship is ₹19,999 for around 10 sessions — this also includes 6 months of Premium Probable Positioning access on the OptionsFlow dashboard, free.
How does group (One-to-Two / One-to-Three / One-to-Four) registration work?
Group pricing is for a group you bring yourself — friends, colleagues, or fellow traders. Once your group of two, three, or four is ready, message the tier you want on WhatsApp, and the details and scheduling will be sorted out from there.
Is the number of sessions always exactly 10?
No — 10 is the general benchmark, not a fixed number. It can wrap up in fewer sessions or extend a bit beyond 10, depending on your pace and depth of interest.
Are the sessions of the Optionsflow Mentorship fixed in time?
No. Each session is scheduled separately based on your availability. There's no batch, no fixed calendar. You can space them out weekly or take them faster — entirely up to you. Sessions don't expire.
Is this SEBI registered advice?
No. These are educational sessions focused on understanding market structure and flow analytics. No investment recommendations are made. All analysis is for educational and informational purposes only.
